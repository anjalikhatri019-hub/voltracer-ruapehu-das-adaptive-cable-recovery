# Novelty audit, not an official score

The nearest identified public work is ORION, *Near real-time channel selection for Distributed Acoustic Sensing technology* ([Seismica](https://seismica.library.mcgill.ca/article/view/2251), DOI 10.26443/seismica.v5i1.2251). ORION selects high-quality channels using spatial clustering and waveform properties to preserve coverage and improve earthquake locations. VOLTRACER shares DAS channel selection, so it would be misleading to call that idea unprecedented. The essential different task is to infer a **single batch-level contingent two-probe policy from only three visible traces**, then score its hidden-channel energy-reconstruction regret and worst-event robustness against all legal policies on real held-out recordings. ORION does not provide this contingent policy program, response-bin replay, reconstruction utility tensor, or tail-risk leaderboard.

Ni et al., *Wavefield Reconstruction of Distributed Acoustic Sensing: Lossy Compression, Wavefield Separation, and Edge Computing* ([JGR: Machine Learning and Computation](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2024JH000247), 2024) studies DAS wavefield reconstruction/compression. VOLTRACER's output is **a sensor acquisition policy**, not reconstructed waveform pixels or samples. It judges a resource-constrained acquisition choice by measured downstream recovery of withheld channels. Existing active sensing and wavefield recovery ideas remain relevant prior art; the claimed innovation is their executable, risk-sensitive benchmark combination on this licensed Ruapehu recording corpus.

This search does not establish that no closer work exists or that ShipD will assign novelty 8/10. The publicly available upstream recording creates a target-lookup risk, documented explicitly in the dataset card and prohibited by challenge rules. If independent review finds a closely matching contingent-policy benchmark or an easy lookup exploit, revise the task mechanism and re-audit rather than changing only novelty wording.
