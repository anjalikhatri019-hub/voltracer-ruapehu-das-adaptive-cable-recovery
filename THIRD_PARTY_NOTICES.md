# Third-party data notice

The real distributed-acoustic-sensing waveforms originate from Leighton Watson, *Distributed Acoustic Sensing at Ruapehu Volcano in 2023*, https://zenodo.org/records/20789299, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20789299, and are licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

VOLTRACER changes: selection of thirteen daily source archives; event grouping and renaming; crop to 14,900 samples; per-event normalization using designated visible channels; signed-int16 quantization; test-channel masking; and derived sensor-response and reconstruction-utility targets. The author of the original data is not claimed to endorse this benchmark. This notice covers the source waveforms; challenge-specific code and prose are separate original work.
